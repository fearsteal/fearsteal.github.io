HOW TO JOIN & RULES

Simple step-by-step instructions for Bedrock (Mobile / Console / PC) and Java editions.

Step-by-Step Connection Instructions
  1. Launch official Minecraft on your PC, iPhone, Android, or Console.
  2. Navigate to Multiplayer → Add Server (or 'Servers' tab on Bedrock).
  3. Enter Server Name: FEARSTEAL
  4. Server Address: play.fearsteal.fun
  5. Port: 19132 (Bedrock port. Java users can leave standard 25565 or default).
  6. Click Join Server and start your LifeSteal conquest!
Official FearSteal Network Rules & Sanctions
  • No Hacked Clients: Any form of fly, killaura, speed, or baritone yields a permanent hardware ban.
  • Fair Conduct: No doxxing, harassment, DDoS threats, discrimination, or staff impersonation.
  • No Glitch Exploitation: Abusing item duplication or chunk crashes results in an inventory purge.
  • Combat Logging: Disconnecting while in combat triggers instant death and drops your inventory + heart.
  • Staff Discretion: Sanctions are finalized by Senior Administrators and Owners.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is FearSteal? FearSteal is a Minecraft network built on high-stakes PvP, custom hearts, clan economies, and regular events.

How do hearts work? Every time you defeat another player, you gain 1 of their max hearts. If you reach 0 hearts, you are temporarily eliminated until revived by a teammate using a Revival Beacon.
