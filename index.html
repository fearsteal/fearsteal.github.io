Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about FearSteal LifeSteal Network.

How do I join on Minecraft Bedrock / PE?

Open Minecraft Bedrock, go to Servers tab, click 'Add Server'. Enter Name: FearSteal, Server Address: play.fearsteal.fun, Port: 19132.

How do I join on Minecraft Java?

In Minecraft Java Edition Multiplayer menu, add Server Address: play.fearsteal.fun (Default port 25565).

How do I claim crate rewards?

Take a screenshot of your won reward on the website, join our Discord server, and send the proof in support chat to claim.

Are modifications allowed?

Hacked clients, X-Ray, Auto-Totem, and macros are strictly prohibited. Permitted mods include OptiFine, Sodium, and minimaps without player radar.
