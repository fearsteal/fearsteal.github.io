Fair Play Enforcement

Network Rules & Policy

Ignorance of rules is not an excuse. Punishments range from warnings to IP bans.

01
No Hacked Clients or Cheating

Auto-clickers above 16 CPS, killaura, fly, speed, or illegal macros result in immediate perm ban.

02
No Bug / Glitch Exploitation

Duplication glitches or phase abuses must be reported to staff. Exploiting will reset inventories.

03
No Harassment or Toxicity

Hate speech, racism, slurs, or real-life threats are strictly zero-tolerance across all platforms.

04
No Doxxing or DDoS Threats

Threatening network infrastructure or revealing private player identity leads to police escalation.

05
No Unauthorized Advertising

Advertising other server IPs, discords, or paid services in public or private chat results in an IP mute.

06
Respect Staff & Community

Staff decisions are final. If you feel mistreated, submit a polite ticket in #support rather than arguing.

Quick Start Guide

How To Connect

Joinable seamlessly from Bedrock (Mobile / Console / Win10) and Java Edition.

STEP 01

Launch Minecraft

Open Minecraft on PC, Mobile (iOS/Android), or Console. Make sure your version is 1.20+.

STEP 02

Multiplayer / Servers

Navigate to the Multiplayer menu (Java) or the "Servers" tab (Bedrock Edition).

STEP 03

Click "Add Server"

Name the server: FearSteal Network to easily find it on your saved server lists.

STEP 04

Enter IP & Port

Server: play.fearsteal.fun
Port: 19132

STEP 05

Click "Join Server"

Connect to our high-speed lobby. The server will authenticate your client automatically.

STEP 06

Begin Stealing Hearts

Select the LifeSteal portal, assemble your clan, and start crafting combat hearts!
