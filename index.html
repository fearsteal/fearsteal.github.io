Code of Conduct

SERVER RULES

Violations will result in temporary or permanent hardware / IP bans.

Any use of modified clients, auto-clickers exceeding 16 CPS, kill-aura, Baritone, fly cheats, speed cheats, or x-ray packs results in an immediate permanent ban without appeal.
Abusing server bugs, duplication glitches, or chunk-loading crashes is strictly prohibited. Report found glitches immediately to staff for in-game bug-bounty rewards.
Death threats, racism, sexual harassment, releasing private information (doxxing), or threatening DDoS attacks will result in an immediate blacklist and forwarding to relevant providers.
Advertising external Minecraft servers, scam links, Discord invite codes, or flooding chat with repeated text will invoke instant mutes or bans.
Staff members have the final say in dispute resolutions. Impersonating staff or arguing excessively over warnings in public chat is grounds for punishment.
