Code of Conduct

FEARSTEAL SERVER RULES

Punishments vary based on violation severity from temporary mutes to hardware bans.

Combat & Gameplay Rules

  • 1. No Hacked Clients: Use of Killaura, Fly, Speed, Baritone, X-Ray, or automated macros is strictly prohibited.
  • 2. No Exploiting Bugs: Duping items, combat logging glitching, or abusing border clipping results in immediate wipe.
  • 3. Fair Combat: Ban evasion or multi-accounting during active combat tag will result in permanent ban.

Community & Chat Conduct

  • 4. No Harassment: Hate speech, racism, threats, and toxic degradation are zero tolerance.
  • 5. No Doxxing or DDoS Threats: Sharing private details or threatening network attacks leads to blacklist and ISP report.
  • 6. Respect Staff: Impersonating staff or arguing over valid tickets will escalate punishment.
Newcomer Guide

HOW TO JOIN & PLAY

Step-by-step connection instructions for Bedrock, PE, and Java editions.

STEP 1

Launch Minecraft

Open Minecraft on Android, iOS, Windows, Xbox, PS, or Switch.

STEP 2

Select Multiplayer

Go to the Servers tab and tap Add Server.

STEP 3

Enter Credentials

IP: play.fearsteal.fun
Port: 19132
STEP 4

Join Server

Save the server entry and click Connect.

STEP 5

Steal Hearts

You spawn with 10 hearts. Kill players to gain hearts. Drop to 0 and you're eliminated!

STEP 6

Create Clans

Use /clan create <name> to form an alliance and build protected bases.
