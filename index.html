KNOWLEDGE BASE & GUIDES
How does LifeSteal Heart elimination work?

When you eliminate an enemy player in any wilderness zone, you permanently steal one heart container (+1 ❤️) while they lose one (-1 ❤️). If a player reaches 0 hearts, they are death-banned for 48 hours or until revived with a crafted Revive Beacon.

How do I craft a Heart Container?

Surround 1 Nether Star with 4 Totems of Undying and 4 Blocks of Diamond in a crafting table. Right click while holding the crafted item to consume it.

What are the base griefing and raid boundaries?

TNT cannons and wither sieges are permitted everywhere except inside spawn protection borders (1,000 blocks radius from 0,0).
