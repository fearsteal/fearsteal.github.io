FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to common questions regarding gameplay and store items.

What is the core premise of FearSteal?
FearSteal is a competitive Minecraft LifeSteal realm. When you eliminate a player, you steal one of their hearts. If you lose all your hearts, you are banned until revived by a teammate or revival beacon!
What is the server address and Bedrock port?
IP: play.fearsteal.fun | Port: 19132 (Bedrock default).
How does the free Novice rank claim work?
The free Novice rank is strictly limited to 100 members over a 60-day window. You must join our official Discord server and verify your IGN. Once 100 slots are claimed, the rank permanently becomes OUT OF STOCK.
How frequently do network events cycle?
Events automatically rotate every 15 days with live countdown trackers for PvP Tournaments, Drop Parties, Boss Fights, and Discord Giveaways.
