SURVIVAL PROTOCOL

HOW LIFESTEAL WORKS

1. PERMANENT HEART THEFT

Whenever you kill an opponent in PvP, you permanently steal 1 of their heart containers. You gain +1 Heart (2 HP), and the victim loses 1 Heart.

2. ELIMINATION & BANISHMENT

If a player's hearts reach 0, they are automatically banished into the Nether Void for 24 hours. Teammates can craft a dark Revival Heart to bring them back.

3. RECIPES

Heart Containers can be forged using 8 Netherite Ingots surrounding an Elytra Crystal. Max heart cap on Season 1 is strictly set to 30 hearts.
