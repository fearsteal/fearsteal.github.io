Code of Conduct

FEARSTEAL SERVER RULES

Strict enforcement is maintained at all times. Punishments vary from temporary mutes to hardware bans.

Gameplay & Combat Rules

  • 1. No Hacked Clients: Use of Killaura, Fly, Speed, Baritone, X-Ray, or automated macros is strictly prohibited.
  • 2. No Exploiting Bugs: Duping items, combat logging glitching, or abusing border clipping results in immediate wipe.
  • 3. Fair Combat: Multi-accounting during active combat combat tag or ban evasion will result in permaban.
  • 4. Event Integrity: Teaming beyond squad limits during solo tournaments will cause instant disqualification.

Community & Chat Conduct

  • 5. No Harassment & Discrimination: Hate speech, racism, threats, and toxic degradation are zero tolerance.
  • 6. No Doxxing or DDoS Threats: Sharing personal information or threatening network attacks leads to blacklist and ISP reporting.
  • 7. No Advertising: Promoting unauthorized Discord invites, external Minecraft IPs, or shops is banned.
  • 8. Respect Staff: Impersonating staff or arguing excessively over valid tickets will escalate punishment.
