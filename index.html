Policy & Fair Play

SERVER RULES

Violations result in permanent blacklist bans without rank refunds.

1. Zero Tolerance for Hacked Clients & Cheating
Any use of auto-clickers, fly hacks, killaura, x-ray packs, baritone, or modified packets will result in immediate permanent automated machine ban without appeal.
2. Bug Exploitation & Duplication
Intentionally duplicating items, utilizing chunk-loading crashes, or bypassing combat log barriers is strictly prohibited. Report exploits immediately for bug bounties.
3. Toxic Behavior, Harassment & Doxxing
Personal threats, real-life doxxing, racism, severe slurs, and DDoS intimidation will be turned over to law enforcement and permanent platform-wide bans.
4. Advertising & Unauthorized Links
Promoting unrelated Discord servers, commercial services, or external Minecraft networks in public chat or direct messages is forbidden.
5. Respect Staff Decisions
Staff members work to maintain network balance. Arguing with moderation actions in general chat is forbidden; use the Discord ticket system for appeals.
Common Inquiries

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about the network, ranks, and mechanics.

What happens when I run out of hearts?
When you lose your final heart, you enter the "Spectator Death Ban" state. You can be revived by clanmates using Heart Revive Beacons or by purchasing an unban revival from the store.
Can Bedrock and Java play together?
Yes! FearSteal utilizes custom cross-play protocol bridges. Java players join via default 25565, while Bedrock/MCPE users connect through port 19132.
How does the Free Novice Rank work?
We offer 100 free Novice ranks on a first-come, first-served basis. Once the 100-member counter fills up, the rank permanently marks as "OUT OF STOCK". Each member is limited to 1 claim.
How often do server events rotate?
Our automated event system operates on an exact 15-day cycle. When the countdown timer hits 0:00:00:00, the previous event wraps up and the next queued event launches instantly.
