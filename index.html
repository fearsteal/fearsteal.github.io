SERVER RULES & CODE OF CONDUCT

Violations result in temporary or permanent hardware & IP bans without refund.

1. Combat & Client Rules

  • ❌ No Hacked Clients: Autoclickers, reach, killaura, fly, or baritone are permanently blacklisted.
  • ❌ No Bug Exploitation: Duping items, combat-logging glitches, or chunk corruption will reset stats.
  • ❌ No Macro / Auto-Totem: Unfair hardware keybinds will result in immediate kick/ban.

2. Community Standards

  • ❌ Zero Harassment: Racism, hate speech, doxxing, and DDoS threats lead to an immediate ban.
  • ❌ No Staff Impersonation: Impersonating ItzVeltriex, Notslayer, or any admin is prohibited.
  • ❌ No External Advertising: Sharing non-FearSteal server IPs or spam links in chat is blocked.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? Find direct answers here

What is the FearSteal IP and Bedrock Port?
The official IP is play.fearsteal.fun and the Bedrock Port is 19132. Cross-play allows Java and Bedrock players to play together smoothly.
How does the free Novice rank promotion work?
The free Novice rank is available for a limited 60-day promotional window. Exactly 100 claims are available. Once the counter reaches 100/100, it marks as OUT OF STOCK. You can claim it once per member.
How often do server events rotate?
Events automatically rotate every 15 days using a synchronized countdown. Once an event concludes, another is selected from the schedule.
