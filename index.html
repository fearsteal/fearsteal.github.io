Answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know about joining and playing FearSteal.

What is FearSteal?
FearSteal is a dedicated Minecraft LifeSteal network focused on competitive gameplay, high-stakes PvP, weekly automated events, and an active player community.
What is the server connection IP and Port?
The server IP address is play.fearsteal.fun and the port is 19132 for both Bedrock and Java players.
How can I join the official Discord?
Click any Discord button on the website or visit directly: discord.gg/pGM5x3gXpX.
How can I purchase a rank or keys?
You can securely browse and purchase all network packages on our official store at store.cryzormc.in.
What happens when the Novice rank reaches 100 claims?
The free Novice rank is strictly limited to 100 players. Once the counter hits 100/100, the button turns into "OUT OF STOCK" and no further free claims can be made.
How often do events rotate?
Events automatically rotate every 15 days on the clock. When one event finishes, the system logs it and schedules the next event in the queue.
