Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is FearSteal?
FearSteal is a premier Minecraft LifeSteal network engineered for intense PvP, balanced economy, clan survival, and community-driven events with crossplay capability.
What is the server IP and Port?
Server IP is play.fearsteal.fun and default port is 19132. Both Bedrock and Java users can connect with this configuration.
How can I join the official Discord?
Click the 'Join Discord' button on this site or visit https://discord.gg/pGM5x3gXpX.
How can I purchase or upgrade a rank?
Visit the official webstore at store.cryzormc.in to purchase ranks and crate keys with instant automatic fulfillment.
How do I apply for Media Rank?
Ensure you meet the subscriber requirements (Smol 200+, Media 500+, Media+ 1000+, Famous 10,000+) and submit an application ticket in the FearSteal Discord channel.
What happens when the free Novice rank reaches 100 claims?
The free Novice rank automatically shifts to 🔴 OUT OF STOCK. Once 100 slots are exhausted, no further free claims can be made.
How often do events rotate?
Events cycle automatically every 15 days, rotating through tournaments, KOTH competitions, boss drops, and community giveaways.
