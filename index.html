COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

FEARSTEAL SERVER RULES

1. Strictly No Cheating or Hacked Clients
Using any third-party modifications that provide an unfair advantage (e.g. X-ray, Fly, KillAura, Auto-Crystal, Baritone, Speed) is strictly forbidden. Players detected using modifications face immediate hardware/IP network bans.
2. No Bug Exploitation or Duplication
Abusing glitches, chunk dupes, combat-log escapes, or inventory-duplication exploits is prohibited. Any discovered bugs must be immediately reported to the staff team for a bug bounty.
3. No DDoS Threats, Doxxing, or Harassment
Threatening to take down the server, boot players offline, releasing personal real-life data, or excessive toxicity will lead to a permanent, unappealable ban from both the Minecraft server and Discord.
4. No Advertising or Impersonation
Promoting other servers, sharing external Discord links without staff approval, or pretending to be a staff member or verified content creator is strictly prohibited.

* Punishments vary based on the severity of the offense. Staff discretion is final.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GOT QUESTIONS?

What is FearSteal?
FearSteal is a competitive Minecraft LifeSteal network designed for Bedrock and Java players. When you eliminate another player, you take one of their maximum hearts. If you lose all your hearts, you are eliminated until revived!
What is the Server IP and Port?
The official server IP is play.fearsteal.fun and the Bedrock default port is 19132. Java players can connect using default port 25565 or play.fearsteal.fun.
How do I claim the Free Novice Rank?
We have allocated exactly 100 free Novice ranks for our early supporters. Scroll to our Store section, click Claim Free Novice Rank, and join our Discord community to verify your Minecraft username.
How frequently do events rotate?
Our automated tournament cycle rotates every 15 days, refreshing leaderboard stats, resetting custom event arenas, and delivering unique prizes.
