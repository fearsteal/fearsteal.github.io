Fair Play

Server Rules

Violations will result in warnings, temporary mutes, or permanent hardware bans.

1

No Cheating or Hacked Clients

X-Ray, Killaura, fly hacks, auto-totem, or modified combat clients trigger an instant ban.

2

No Exploiting or Glitch Abuse

Duplication glitches or breaking bedrock barriers must be reported immediately to staff.

3

No Harassment or Discrimination

Racism, hate speech, severe toxicity, and personal harassment are strictly forbidden.

4

No Spam or Advertising

Promoting other servers, YouTube channels without media rank, or spamming chat will lead to a mute.

5

No DDoS Threats or Doxxing

Attempting to trace IPs, leak personal identities, or threaten network uptime results in a permanent blacklist.

6

Respect Staff & Community

Follow moderator directions during events and do not impersonate staff or creators.
