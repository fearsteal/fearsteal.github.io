Fair Play & Community Guidelines

SERVER RULES

01. No Cheating, Hacked Clients, or Unfair Advantages
Any client modifications giving unfair movement, aura, or automated advantages result in immediate network IP bans without appeal opportunities.
02. No Exploiting Glitches or Duplication Bugs
Exploiting heart duplication, game tick abuse, or inventory corruption is strictly forbidden. Report bugs directly to staff for reward bounties.
03. Zero Tolerance for Harassment, Doxxing & DDoS Threats
Any cyber threats, disclosure of private personal information (doxxing), hate speech, or DDoS threats lead to an instant permanent blacklist.
04. No Advertising or Impersonation
Do not promote other servers, websites, or external Discord servers. Impersonating staff members or content creators will result in a mute or ban.
05. Staff Discretion Policy
Punishments may vary based on the severity of the infraction. Staff decisions regarding toxicity, exploit prevention, and chat flow are final.

Common Inquiries

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is FearSteal?
FearSteal is a competitive Minecraft LifeSteal network featuring custom hearts, heart crafting, seasonal leaderboards, automated PvP events, and zero pay-to-win mechanics.
What is the exact server IP & Port?
IP: play.fearsteal.fun | Port: 19132. Both Java and Bedrock clients can connect through this address.
How does the free Novice rank claim work?
Only the first 100 unique players can claim the Novice rank. Once 100/100 claims are reached, the button locks to 'OUT OF STOCK'. Each registered account can claim it only once.
How frequently do events rotate?
Events rotate automatically every 15 days via our scheduled rotation engine, alternating between in-game Minecraft tournaments and Discord community challenges.
