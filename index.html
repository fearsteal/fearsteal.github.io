NETWORK LAW & GUIDELINES

FEARSTEAL CODE OF CONDUCT

Violations will result in warnings, mutes, temporary bans or permanent IP blacklists.

Any client modifications giving combat or movement advantages (Killaura, Fly, Speed, Auto-Totem, Reach, Baritone, X-Ray) are strictly banned.

Punishment: 30-day ban on 1st offense. Permanent blacklist on 2nd offense.

Logging out while in combat will spawn an NPC or automatically execute you, forfeiting 1 heart to the attacker.

Punishment: Instant combat death + 12-hour combat cooldown.

Using alternate Minecraft accounts to repeatedly kill for free hearts is classified as exploit farming. Our anti-abuse engine logs UUID kill transfers.

Punishment: Complete heart reset to 10 hearts + permanent ban for alts.

Severe harassment, racism, doxxing, death threats, or posting links to other Minecraft networks is prohibited.

Punishment: 1-hour mute up to permanent network blacklist.
