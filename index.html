SERVER RULES

Violations result in automated IP & hardware blacklist. Play with honor.

01 NO UNFAIR ADVANTAGE / CHEATING
▼
Using hacked clients, auto-clickers, baritone, macros, or combat modifications gives an illegal advantage. We use advanced enterprise packet monitoring to detect velocity, reach, and killaura.
PUNISHMENT: Permanent Network Ban with zero appeal rights.
02 NO COMBAT LOGGING OR EXPLOITING
▼
Disconnecting during PvP combat will trigger instant death and drop all inventory including active heart containers. Duplication glitches or Ender Pearl phase glitching through claims is strictly prohibited.
PUNISHMENT: 14-day temporary ban + inventory/heart wipe.
03 TOXICITY, DOXING & HARASSMENT
▼
Competitive banter is welcome; however, death threats, hate speech, doxing, and DDoS threats will not be tolerated under any circumstance.
PUNISHMENT: Permanent mute progressing to permanent network IP ban.
04 LIFESTEAL HEART BOOSTING
▼
Killing alternate accounts (alts), cooperating with friends to artificially inflate heart counts, or selling hearts for real-world currency outside the official FearSteal Store is forbidden.
PUNISHMENT: Heart reset to base 10 hearts + 30-day ban.
