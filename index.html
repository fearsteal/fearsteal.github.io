ABOUT FEARSTEAL

FearSteal is a Minecraft network created for players who want competitive PvP, exciting survival gameplay and a strong community. From Lifesteal and Practice to Survival, BoxPvP and TierTest, FearSteal is built around one idea: Enter the Fear. Rule the Lifesteal.

The network is developed and managed by ItzVeltriex, with a focus on creating a premium Minecraft experience.
