Code of Conduct

FEARSTEAL RULES

Punishments vary depending on severity and prior record. Respect all players.

RULE #01

No Hacked Clients or Cheats

Use of Killaura, Baritone, Fly, Auto-Totem, Jesus, or modified reach will result in an immediate permanent hardware ban.

RULE #02

No Bug or Glitch Exploitation

Duplication exploits, phase glitching through bedrock, or infinite heart glitches must be reported directly to staff for rewards.

RULE #03

Zero Harassment & Hate Speech

Racism, discrimination, sexual harassment, or derogatory slurs in public chat or signs will incur an immediate mute or ban.

RULE #04

No DDoS, Doxxing or Threats

Real-life threats, IP threats, or posting personal identifiable information will result in legal escalation and permaban.

RULE #05

No Staff Impersonation

Claiming to be ItzVeltriex, Notslayer, or any Moderator in-game or on Discord is strictly forbidden.

RULE #06

No External Advertising

Promoting unapproved Discord links, other Minecraft server IPs, or monetized external services is not permitted.
