SERVER RULES

Strict compliance is required. Violations will result in permanent bans, IP blocks, or rank forfeits.

No Cheating: Using hacked clients, X-ray, or unauthorized mods is strictly banned.
No Bug Exploiting: Duping or taking advantage of server glitches will result in wipes.
No Harassment: Keep gameplay competitive without toxic hate speech or bullying.
No Discrimination: Zero tolerance for racism, homophobia, sexism or bigotry.
No Inappropriate Content: NSFW skins, builds, or chat links are forbidden.
No Chat Spam: Excessive caps, chat flooding, or repetitive adverts will be muted.
No Advertising: Mentioning or promoting other Minecraft servers will lead to an IP ban.
No DDoS Threats: Malicious threat of attacks will be immediately forwarded to hosts.
No Doxxing: Sharing private identity or sensitive personal data is strictly illegal.
No Impersonation: Pretending to be FearSteal staff or content creators is penalized.
No Glitch Abuse: Unfairly manipulating border or combat-tag timers is prohibited.
Respect Staff: Follow decisions made by staff members without abusive arguments.
Follow Event Rules: Cheating or teaming in solo-only tournaments leads to disqualification.
Follow Discord Rules: Maintain civil etiquette inside all official discord channels.
Minecraft EULA: Comply with standard Mojang EULA requirements at all times.
Fair Gameplay: Ban evasion or using alt accounts to bypass death bans is disallowed.
