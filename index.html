NETWORK RULES & CONDUCT

Violating rules results in mutes, temporary bans or blacklists.

01 — NO CHEATING / HACKED CLIENTS +
Using combat hacks (Killaura, Reach, Velocity), movement hacks (Fly, Speed), or auto-clickers above 16 CPS is strictly prohibited. Permanent network ban.
02 — NO EXPLOITING / DUPING +
Any duplication glitch, portal traps preventing logout, or unlinked heart transfer exploits will trigger an immediate inventory wipe and ban.
03 — RESPECT & ZERO HARASSMENT +
Toxic slurs, racism, doxxing, and DDoS threats have zero tolerance. Offending players are blacklisted immediately.
04 — NO SPAM & NO ADVERTISING +
Do not post links to external Minecraft servers or phishing sites in public or private messages.
05 — OBEY STAFF INSTRUCTIONS +
Staff members enforce rules impartially. If you believe a punishment was unfair, submit an appeal rather than spamming chat.
