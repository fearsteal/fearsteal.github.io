Strict Enforcement

Server Rules & Conduct

Prohibited Actions

  • 1. No Hacked Clients: Autoclickers, X-ray, fly, speed or killaura result in immediate permanent bans.
  • 2. No Exploiting Glitches: Duping items, glitching through bedrock, or using server bugs is forbidden.
  • 3. No Harassment & Toxicity: Slurs, extreme toxicity, racism, and hateful speech are zero tolerance.
  • 4. No Impersonation: Impersonating staff members or content creators leads to severe sanctions.
  • 5. No Doxxing or DDoS Threats: Threatening to DDoS players or posting private personal data yields instant blacklist.

Fairplay & Penalties

  • • Respect Staff Decisions: Staff verdicts are final during live events and disputes.
  • • Punishment Scaling: Violations escalate: Warning → 7 Day Ban → 30 Day Ban → Permanent Blacklist.
  • • Discord Alignment: Server rules also apply strictly to official Discord text & voice lobbies.
  • • Appeals: Submit honest punishment appeals in our designated Discord support tickets.
