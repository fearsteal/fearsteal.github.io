The Heart Economy

Every player enters FearSteal with a baseline of 10 Hearts (20 HP). The absolute maximum reachable is 20 Hearts (40 HP).

✓ Kill a player: +1 Heart permanently
✗ Die by player: -1 Heart dropped as item
⚠ Reach 0 Hearts: Permanent elimination / Ban until revived

Revival Beacon

When a faction mate is banned after hitting 0 hearts, construct a Revival Altar. Use the altar with 4 Netherite Blocks and 1 Nether Star to resurrect them with 1 starter heart!

/revive <username> consumes the crafted altar and triggers a global thunder strike across the entire server.
