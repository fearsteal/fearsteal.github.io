FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does the Heart Stealing mechanic work?
When you deliver the final blow to an enemy player in any wilderness or warzone area, 1 heart item will drop or transfer directly to your profile. The victim loses 1 max heart permanently until they forge or buy another.
What occurs when a player reaches 0 hearts?
Reaching zero hearts triggers the Death Ban (12 hour timeout in purgatory) or Spectator state, unless revived by a teammate using a Revive Beacon.
Can Bedrock Edition players link their accounts?
Yes! Bedrock players connect using the same IP play.fearsteal.net with default port 19132. Prefix your gamertag with an asterisk or link via Discord.
