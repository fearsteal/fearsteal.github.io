Fair Play Policies

SERVER RULES & GUIDELINES

Violations carry penalties ranging from temporary mutes to permanent hardware bans.

1. No Cheating or Unfair Clients

Hacked clients (Baritone, X-Ray packs, Killaura, Speed, Auto-Totem) are strictly prohibited and detected instantly by our anticheat.

2. No Glitch or Dupe Exploits

Exploiting bugs, bedrock block breaking glitches, or item dupes will result in immediate data wipe and permanent blacklisting.

3. Respect Staff & Players

Harassment, racism, hate speech, doxxing, DDoS threats, and staff impersonation are treated with zero tolerance.

4. No Unauthorized Advertising

Promoting other servers, YouTube channels not partnered with FearSteal, or sending phishing links is instantly blocked.

5. Follow Event Rules

Teaming in solo tournaments or cross-teaming will lead to immediate forfeiture of rewards and disqualification.

6. Discord Community Code

Server rules extend across all voice channels and text chats within our official Discord server.
