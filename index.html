Player Guide

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join using Java Edition? +
Open Minecraft Java Edition → Multiplayer → Add Server. Use play.fearsteal.fun with port 25565.
Can Bedrock players join? +
Yes. Use play.fearsteal.fun with Bedrock port 19132.
How does Lifesteal work? +
Your hearts are an important survival resource. PvP encounters can affect your heart count according to FearSteal's Lifesteal mechanics.
How does the store work? +
Ranks and other benefits can be purchased through the official FearSteal store.
When does the server wipe? +
Season and world reset announcements are posted through the official FearSteal Discord.
How can I contact staff? +
Join the official Discord and open the appropriate support ticket.
