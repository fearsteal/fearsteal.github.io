Network Regulations

FearSteal Server Rules

Violations result in temporary kicks, permanent bans, or blacklists.

1. No Cheating & Hacked Clients

Killaura, fly, x-ray, reach, baritone, auto-totem or any unauthorized injection mods are strictly prohibited.

2. No Exploiting Glitches

Duplication, item unstacking glitches, phase glitches, and pearl glitches must be reported immediately.

3. No Harassment or Hate Speech

Zero tolerance for toxicity, racism, bigotry, real-life threats, dox threats, or blackmail.

4. No Doxxing or DDoS Threats

Threatening network infrastructure or revealing personally identifiable player data causes immediate blacklist.

5. No Advertising & Spam

Advertising external servers, malicious links, or spamming public chat triggers automated mute filters.

6. Respect Staff & Server

Do not impersonate staff or attempt to deceive network moderators during support investigations.
